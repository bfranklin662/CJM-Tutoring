✨ My Ethos

Hello, I'm Camilla. I live with my Spanish partner and have two grown-up children who are now university students. I spend my time between my home in Spain and the South West of England.

I believe in calm, confidence-building tuition that empowers every student to reach their full potential. My lessons are interactive, encouraging, and completely tailored to the learner’s needs.

Everyone is different and I take time to get to know my students. I firmly believe that every student can reach their potential if they are determined and I see my role as a facilitator. As an English & Drama specialist, I am an expert at helping students to develop strong communication skills which are essential for their way forward in this world.

🎓 Teaching Background

I studied at Bristol and Warwick universities and have over 28 years of experience teaching KS3, GCSE, and A Level English & Drama both in the UK and internationally. I am DBS certified and have been a Head of Department and Edexcel examiner.

Over the years, I have worked with all major exam boards and helped students achieve outstanding GCSE and A-Level results. Some of my students have even won prestigious Pearson awards in consecutive years for achieving the highest GCSE grades in Europe.

I also have extensive experience teaching English as a second language to a wide range of ages, from children to adults. I have prepared students for the Cambridge exams B2, C1 & C2 with a strong record of success.
